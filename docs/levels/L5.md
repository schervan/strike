# L5 – OVERMIND Core + boss

id `l5` · theme `overmind_core` · music `music_core` (boss: `music_boss`) · par 900 s (M4-1, was 780; the time runs through the boss arena, ≈ 2 min of boss) · ★3 rescues ≤ 2 · fallbackSeed 5005

**Mood:** purple-gold energy halls, floating cubes, a giant glowing core. OVERMIND's face is
everywhere, making funny angry faces.

**Structure:** generated part (2 zones) → exit door → fixed boss arena (§9 GDD) → ending.

## Boss fight (see GDD §9)

Phase 1 Stomp (1200→800) · Phase 2 Shield + core puzzle (800→400) · Phase 3 Overload (400→0).
Rescue (QA-016, owner decision): the boss keeps its damage, and only its attack timing resets.
Cores stay inserted.

M4-1 check (GDD §8.3): damage time at kid aim 72 s (spread) to 93 s (starter only), plus ≈ 25 s
core puzzle and ≈ 15 s of lines. With rescues: ≈ 2.1–2.8 min for good and average kids, and
always finishes. Pillar pickups (QA-019: + 1 `ammo_starter`) give 569 kid-damage per phase,
1,707 over 3 phases ≥ 1,200 HP.
